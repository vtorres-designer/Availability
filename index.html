Add Schedule

Paste Your Schedule

One line per day or date range. Lines further down win if two lines cover the same day.

How to Write Each Line 
Fri 10/9 2300-0700 "Site B"
Sat 10/10 2300-0700 "Site B"
Sun 10/11 no shift
Mon 10/12 2200-0600 "Site C, Gate 2"
Tue 10/13 no shift
Wed 10/14 busy
Thu 10/15 2300-0700
  • One line per day: day name (optional), date, then the shift. The day name is checked against the date, which catches typos.
  • Working: start-end in 24-hour time, like 2300-0700. A shift that ends after midnight still goes on the day it starts. Paid hours come from the times. If a break isn't paid, add the paid hours at the end, like 7.5h.
  • Not scheduled: no shift. The day turns green, or yellow if picking up a shift would mean any overtime.
  • Don't want to work: busy (red). off alone is flagged because it could mean either.
  • Notes go in quotes: 2300-0700 "Site B". Anything in quotes is a private note and is never read as a time or a word like open or clear. To quote something inside a note, use single quotes: "Sgt said 'call me'". Any other word the site doesn't recognize is flagged.
  • Every-week days from Settings stay red unless a line here says otherwise for that date. clear puts a date back to its every-week setting.
  • Also works: 10/7, Oct 7, 2026-10-07, ranges (10/12-10/14 no shift), lists (10/9, 10/10 2300-0700), 23:00-07:00 or 11pm-7am, clear to make a day gray again.
  • Pick days in a range with day names right after the dates: 10/12-10/25 Mon-Fri 2300-0700, Mon Wed Fri, M/W/F, TTh, weekdays or weekends.
  • If a line could mean two things, it's listed with the reason instead of guessed. Reword it and it'll be added.

One More Step

        
Keep Your Hours Private

      

      
    

    
Your times, hours and notes are saved in a private spot in your repository, not in the public calendar file. Your GitHub key needs permission to use it. You don't need to paste the key again.

    
    
      
  1. Open GitHub → Fine-Grained Tokens and tap Availability calendar.
    2. 
      
  2. Tap Edit. Under Repository access, check that Availability is selected.
    3. 
      
  3. Under Repository permissions, set Variables to Read and write (not Read-only).
    4. 
      
  4. Tap Update, come back here, and tap Try Again.
    5. 
    

    
Until then you can look at your calendar, but you can't change it, so nothing you do here gets lost.
